How a month works

One shoot day covers the whole month

We film in batches, so we are in your shop once a month, not every week.

  1. Before the month

    Plan together

    We write a content calendar and scripts in your chosen language. You approve them before we film anything.

  2. Week 1

    Shoot day

    Joshua films and Dhathri presents. One visit gives us 8 to 12 videos.

  3. Week 2

    Edit

    Hemanth cuts the footage, adds captions and sends you drafts. We ask for feedback within 48 hours.

  4. Week 3

    Revise and post

    We make your changes, then scheduling and posting begin.

  5. Week 4

    Report

    We watch performance, reply to comments and share what worked, plus ideas for next month.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Which areas do you work in?

Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Tell us your town when you get in touch. If your shop is far from us, we confirm any travel cost on the first call.

ఆంధ్రప్రదేశ్, తెలంగాణ రెండింటిలోనూ పని చేస్తాం. మీ ఊరు చెప్పండి. దూరం ఎక్కువైతే ప్రయాణ ఖర్చు ముందే చెబుతాం.

Which languages can you make videos in?

Telugu, English and Tinglish, which is Telugu and English mixed. You choose the language for each video, and captions match it.

తెలుగు, ఇంగ్లీష్, తెలుగు-ఇంగ్లీష్ మిక్స్ (Tinglish). ప్రతి వీడియోకి మీరు భాష ఎంచుకోవచ్చు.

What is the free sample Reel?

One short vertical video made for your business, so you can judge our work on your own shop before deciding anything. There is no obligation.

How much will it cost?

Packages start at ₹8,000 a month for videos and ₹12,000 for a website. After a short call we confirm the exact price. Extra videos, added revisions and on-location shoots cost extra.

How often do you need to visit my shop?

Usually once a month. One shoot day gives us enough footage for 8 to 12 videos.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, photos and brand details, plus access to your social pages. Admin access through Meta Business Suite works if you'd rather not share logins.

How does payment work?

Monthly packages are paid in advance. Websites are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery.

Who pays for ads?

You do. The ad budget goes directly to Meta. Our fee covers setting up and managing the ads in the Growth package.

Who owns the videos and the website?

After full payment, the finished videos and website are yours to use for your business. We keep the raw footage and project files, and we may show the work in our portfolio unless you tell us in writing that you'd rather we didn't.

Can you promise results?

No. We can't promise followers or sales. Every month we share views, reach, followers and enquiries, and tell you what we'd do next.
